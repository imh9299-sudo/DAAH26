## Assignment 1: Storyboard

Create a detailed storyboard for a short film or video project. Your storyboard should include:

- **Scene descriptions** – Brief narrative for each scene
- **Visual composition** – Sketch or describe camera angles, framing, and shot types
- **Transitions** – How scenes connect to one another
- **Audio notes** – Dialogue, sound effects, or music cues
- **Timing** – Duration or pacing for each scene

### Submission Guidelines

- Format: PDF, image sequence, or digital storyboard tool (Storyboarder, Milanote, etc.)
- Minimum: 8–10 scenes
- Due: [Insert due date here]

### Resources

- [Storyboarder](https://www.storyboarder.com/) – Free open-source storyboarding tool
- [Frame.io](https://frame.io/) – Collaboration and review platform
- [Shot Designer](https://www.shotdesigner.com/) – Virtual storyboarding

Good luck with your assignment!
