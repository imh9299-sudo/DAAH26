## Assignment 2: Data Analysis Project

Create a comprehensive data analysis project that explores a topic of interest. Your analysis should include:

- **Research Question** – A clear, specific question you want to explore
- **Data Collection** – Describe your data sources and collection methods
- **Data Visualization** – Include charts, graphs, or other visual representations
- **Analysis & Findings** – Interpret your results and explain what they mean
- **Methodology** – Document your tools and processes used

### Submission Guidelines

- Format: Written report (PDF or Markdown) with embedded visualizations
- Minimum: 5 pages of analysis with at least 3 data visualizations
- Tools: You may use Python, R, Excel, Tableau, or other analysis tools
- Due: [Insert due date here]
